# P-H3-Image 三面図参照・最終プロンプト集

三面図を `Picture 1` に入力し、キャラクターの顔・髪型・画風を参照しながら、新しい場面を生成するためのプロンプトです。

- **制服学校**：手描き2Dアニメ風の教室で勉強中（教室の検証で使用した最終版）
- **セーター振り返り**：繊細なアニメイラスト調、ニットの質感を改善した版
- **ビキニ砂浜**：セーターの検証で得た知見を反映した改訂版

## 1. 制服学校

```text
Edit <Picture 1>.

The finished image is a single frame from a hand-drawn Japanese 2D television anime series.

A single young woman is sitting at a school desk in a Japanese classroom, writing in an open notebook. She wears a white and navy sailor school uniform.

The camera shows her from a three-quarter side angle, with her face clearly visible. Soft afternoon sunlight enters through the classroom windows from the right.

The entire scene uses traditional 2D anime artwork. Both the character and the classroom background have clean drawn outlines, flat matte colors, simplified shapes, and two-tone cel shading. The wooden desks, chairs, windows, and walls are drawn as flat-colored anime background art with simplified surface details and broad painted shadow shapes.

Use <Picture 1> only as the reference for the woman's character identity and drawing style. Preserve her facial features, gray-violet eyes, dark updo hairstyle, straight bangs, and loose side locks.

The finished image contains one character in one classroom scene, rather than the original three-view diagram.
```
